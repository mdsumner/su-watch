SU watch

Nectar Research Cloud Service Unit budgets, use and burn, for every project I belong to. Updated daily.

!

Budget used

on track runs out early, or over 80% over budget or ended share of the allocation period gone

Stopped is not off. Shut-off and suspended VMs are charged at the full rate. Only shelving or deleting a VM stops the charge (Nectar: Service Units).

Budget: the current approved allocation's SU budget. Used: SU charged since that allocation started (Nectar Rating service). Burn: the flavour rate of every VM that exists now. Runs out: when the budget is gone at that burn.
